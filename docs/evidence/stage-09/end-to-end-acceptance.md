# End-to-end lifecycle acceptance

The cumulative application and repository suites cover the plan's 14-step
scenario without direct canonical editing:

1. JSON/CSV/ReqIF preview and commit report transformations, rejections and
   duplicates, then allocate or preserve governed IDs.
2. Exact ID, text, attribute, baseline and coverage searches stay bounded.
3. Manager-only link/reassess commands repair invalid, missing and suspect
   traceability while read-only roles are denied mutation.
4. Reviews freeze exact versions; readiness gates and baseline checksums bind
   requirements, relationships, policy and approvals.
5. Controlled changes analyze bounded impact across internal and external
   artifacts, disposition impacts, record decisions, and atomically implement.
6. Material updates invalidate evidence and mark configured links suspect until
   explicit reassessment.
7. Verification plans and accepted evidence bind exact revised versions.
8. Specification, RTM, coverage, change, comparison, readiness and audit reports
   are reproducible and expose drill-down populations.
9. Backup/isolated restore reproduces canonical, baseline and audit integrity;
   the index is discarded and rebuilt.
10. The Stage 9 benchmark repeats the highest-cost operations at 25 times the
    signed-off Year 3 requirement count and 50,005 relationships.

Negative paths include unauthorized calls, stale versions/revisions, rejected
import rows, duplicate integration delivery keys, failed verification, waivers,
suspect links, truncated impact, corrupt indexes, audit tampering, and every
crash point during commit. The role-matrix suite covers requirements manager,
tester, implementer, and reviewer. Operations and security workflows are
covered by integrity, health, support-bundle, recovery and security suites.

Human usability/accessibility review and representative pilot feedback cannot
be manufactured by automation. Their signature fields remain explicitly
pending in the production readiness checklist.
